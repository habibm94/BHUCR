# C1 — Shop cash ledger (complex reasoning)

A shop's daily handwritten ledger: opening cash, cash sales, dues, receivables, payables, side notes on the right edge, crossed-out edits and arrows.
Question: how much cash will be in the drawer at closing tonight? Answer type: number. Answer: 22890.

Complexity: 12 rules → complex (more than 3).

Why it is hard:
1. Handwritten Bengali digits (17000 can be misread as 1700, ৩ as ৫).
2. Crossed-out text cancels or changes a condition (Abdullah, Osman, Omar, Shyama).
3. Each amount must be placed by time: today, tonight, tomorrow, day after, next month.
4. Netting: Jamil owes 300, is owed 200, pays 100 tonight.
5. Side notes on the right edge are easy to miss (Chaiti, Swarna).

Rubric: only items that change tonight's cash (15 reading, 5 reasoning). Full rubric: task_en.md.
